YouTube Playlist Search

When you save a video, get a searchable list of every playlist you own — including the ones YouTube's own picker can't reach.

1

Grant access to youtube.com

So the extension can see when you click Save and load your playlists from YouTube. It runs only on youtube.com, adds nothing to YouTube's own page, and talks only to YouTube, as you — never to the developer or any third party.

2

Save a video to see it in action

Open any video on YouTube and click Save. Instead of YouTube's picker you'll get a search field — start typing and the list narrows to what you meant. Nothing to scroll.

Where you'll see it

  • Save on a video page, or ⋮ → Save to playlist anywhere else — the home feed, search results, a channel, your subscriptions or history
  • The toolbar icon, for anything with no Save button of its own — Shorts, for instance
  • Right-click any video link → Save to playlist
  • Alt+S on a video page
Why it has all of them: YouTube's own Save picker loads at most 200 playlists, and there is no way to ask it for more. This reads your library from a different endpoint that isn't capped, so every playlist you own is searchable. One honest caveat: past that same 200, YouTube won't tell anyone which playlists already contain a video — so those rows are shown unmarked rather than guessed at.
