YouTube Playlist Search

The extension will not work until you allow YouTube access.

It runs on youtube.com using your existing YouTube sign-in. Your data is not sent to third parties.

Choose Allow in Chrome’s permission prompt.

Open a fresh YouTube tab below, or reload any YouTube tab you already had open. Setup won’t take effect in an old tab until you reload it.

Sign in to the YouTube account with your playlists. On a video, click Save → search → pick a playlist.
