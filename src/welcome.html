YouTube Playlist Search v—

Type, don’t scroll.

Saving a video opens a search field over every playlist you own, including the ones YouTube’s own picker can’t reach.

  1. 1

    Grant access to youtube.com

    The extension does nothing at all until you allow it. It runs only on youtube.com, draws its own interface instead of touching YouTube’s page, ships no third-party code, and stores nothing. It talks to YouTube as you, and to no one else.

  2. 2

    Save a video and watch it change

    Open anything on YouTube and hit Save. Instead of the usual picker you get a search field: three letters and the list is already down to what you meant.

Where you’ll see it

Save
On a video page. Everywhere else, including the home feed, search results, a channel, your subscriptions and your history, open the overflow menu and choose Save to playlist.
Toolbar icon
For anything with no Save button of its own. Shorts, for instance.
Right-click
Any video link, then Save to playlist.
Alt + S
On a video page.

The 200 limit

YouTube’s own Save picker loads at most 200 playlists and offers no way to ask it for more. This reads your library from a different endpoint that isn’t capped, so all of them are searchable. Past that same 200, though, YouTube won’t tell anyone (us included) which playlists already contain a video. Those rows are shown unmarked rather than guessed at.
