YouTube Playlist Search

Less scrolling.
More saving.

Search all the playlists you own, right in YouTube’s Save menu.

YouTube access is turned off

The extension cannot work while YouTube site access is restricted. Restore access to search your playlists.

If Chrome asks, choose Allow. If access stays off, go to Chrome’s Extensions → YouTube Playlist Search → Site access and allow access to www.youtube.com.

You’re welcome to jump right in. There’s no extra setup on this page.

This opens a fresh tab. For tabs already open, reload YouTube first.

Your first save, in three small steps.

  1. Find a video you like

    Sign in to the YouTube account with your playlists, then open a video.

  2. Choose Save

    Under the video, click Save. It may be inside YouTube’s three-dot menu.

  3. Search, then pick

    Type a playlist name in the Save sheet. Choose the playlist to save your video.

Optional shortcuts: Alt+S to save (Shorts too), Alt+P to find a playlist.

Keep it handy (optional)

Open Chrome’s Extensions puzzle menu and pin YouTube Playlist Search. On YouTube, click its icon to save the current video or find a playlist.
