How to find your playlists on YouTube (and the ones that went missing)

By Cody Hergenroeder, who builds YouTube Playlist Search and reads YouTube's playlist API for a living · Updated October 23, 2026

Quick answer

On a computer, go to youtube.com/feed/playlists, or open the menu (☰) and click Playlists under You. In the YouTube app on Android or iPhone, tap You (your profile picture, bottom right), then tap View all next to Playlists. In YouTube Music, tap Library and pick the Playlists filter. If a playlist still isn't there, you are probably signed in to a different account or channel.

How do I find my playlists on YouTube? Desktop

The fastest way is the address bar. Bookmark this and you never have to click through a menu again:

youtube.com/feed/playlists

If you'd rather click:

  1. Open youtube.com and make sure you're signed in (your profile picture is top right, not a Sign in button).
  2. Click the menu icon (☰) at the top left if the sidebar is collapsed.
  3. Under the You section, click Playlists.

That page holds everything: playlists you made, playlists you saved from other channels, Watch later, and Liked videos. When I checked it this week, the top of the page had a sort menu set to A-Z and filter chips for Playlists, Music, Courses, Owned and Saved. YouTube's other sort option is Recently added. Those filters are the most useful thing on the page and almost nobody notices them.

How to find your playlists in the YouTube app Android iPhone

The old Library tab is gone. Its contents moved into the You tab, which is your profile picture in the bottom right corner. Same steps on Android, iPhone and iPad:

  1. Open the YouTube app and tap You (your profile picture, bottom right).
  2. Scroll to the Playlists row. It's a sideways carousel that only shows a handful.
  3. Tap View all next to the Playlists heading to see the full list, then use the sort option (Recently added or A-Z) if it's long.

On the mobile website (m.youtube.com) it's the same idea: tap your profile picture, and your playlists are listed there.

The carousel is not the list. A lot of "my playlist disappeared" panic comes from the You tab showing only your recent playlists. The rest are behind View all.

How do I find my playlists on YouTube Music? Music

  1. In the YouTube Music app, tap Library at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap the Playlists filter at the top of your library.
  3. On a computer, your playlists also show in the left sidebar at music.youtube.com, or go straight to music.youtube.com/library/playlists.

YouTube and YouTube Music share one set of playlists. A playlist you make in either app shows up in both. The catch, per YouTube's own help page: YouTube Music only shows the music videos from a playlist. Your "woodworking tutorials" playlist will look empty or missing in Music. It's fine. Open it on YouTube.

How to find a specific playlist on YouTube

Fine with 15 playlists. Annoying with 300. I have a few hundred, which is why I ended up writing software about it.

One of your own playlists Desktop

  1. Go to youtube.com/feed/playlists.
  2. Click Owned to hide everything you saved from other people.
  3. Set the sort to A-Z if you know how the name starts, or Recently added if you used it lately.
  4. Scroll to the bottom until no more playlists load. The page loads in batches, and the browser can only search what's loaded.
  5. Press Ctrl+F (⌘+F on a Mac) and type part of the name.

Long term, naming helps more than any tool. Prefixes like music /, learn /, recipes / group related playlists together under A-Z sort.

Someone else's playlist

  • Search with the Playlist filter. Search YouTube, click Filters, and choose Playlist under the result type. Now every result is a playlist, not a video.
  • Check the channel. Open the creator's channel and look at its Playlists tab.
  • Search only finds public playlists. A private playlist is invisible to everyone but its owner, and an unlisted one only opens from its link.
Press Alt+P (Option+P on Mac), type three letters, done

Why can't I find my playlist on YouTube?

Work down this list. In my experience the first one explains most cases.

1. You're on the wrong account or channel

Playlists belong to a channel, not just to your Google login. If you have a Brand Account (a second channel under the same login), it has its own playlists, and switching to it makes your personal ones look gone. Click your profile picture, choose Switch account, and check each channel listed. If you're signed in to more than one Google account in the same browser, check those too.

2. You're signed out

Signed out, the You tab and the Playlists page have nothing to show. This catches people most in private windows, on a new device, or on a shared computer.

3. It's on your Playlists page but not in the Save menu

This is the one nobody explains, so here's what I found while building my extension. YouTube's Save to playlist menu loads at most 200 playlists. I tested it against YouTube's own API on an account with over 250: no scrolling, no "show more", no setting changes it. Playlist 201 isn't further down the list. It isn't in the list at all.

Which 200 you get appears to be your most recently updated playlists, with Watch later always first. So the old playlist you haven't touched in a year can exist on /feed/playlists and be absent from Save. The menu also never shows playlists you saved from other people, or Liked videos, because you can't add to those.

Workarounds without installing anything: save the video to Watch later now and sort it out later, or open the old playlist and edit it (reorder a video, change its description). Since the menu seems to favor recently updated playlists, that may pull it back into the 200. That last part is my inference from testing, not something YouTube documents. 4. The owner deleted it or made it private

If it was a playlist you saved, not one you made, you were only ever holding a link to someone else's playlist. When the owner deletes it or makes it private, it stops working for you. YouTube doesn't keep a copy for you, so there's nothing to restore. If a playlist matters, make your own copy of it.

5. You're looking in YouTube Music

As above, YouTube Music only shows music videos. A playlist of anything else can look empty or missing there. Check on YouTube instead.

6. It was deleted

Deleting a playlist on YouTube asks you to confirm and then it's gone. I don't know of any way to bring a deleted playlist back from YouTube's side, so check the five causes above before you assume this one.

Questions people ask

Why can't I find a playlist on YouTube?

The usual causes, in order: you are signed in to a different account or a different channel (playlists belong to a channel, so a Brand Account has its own set), you are signed out, the playlist was saved from someone who has since deleted it or made it private, or you are looking in YouTube Music, which only shows the music videos from your playlists. If the playlist is on youtube.com/feed/playlists but missing from the Save menu, that is YouTube's Save menu only loading 200 playlists.

Where did my playlist go?

Check youtube.com/feed/playlists on a computer first, with the Owned or Saved filter, while signed in to the right account and channel. If it is there, it never left; it is just not in the menu you were looking at. If it was a playlist you saved from another creator and it is gone, the creator most likely deleted it or made it private. If you deleted it yourself, I don't know of any way to restore it from YouTube's side.

How to find a specific playlist on YouTube?

For one of your own, open youtube.com/feed/playlists, sort A-Z, scroll to the bottom so every playlist loads, then press Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on a Mac) and type part of the name. For someone else's public playlist, search YouTube, click Filters, and choose Playlist under the result type, or open the creator's channel and check its Playlists tab.

How to get on YouTube playlists?

To get to your playlists, go to youtube.com/feed/playlists on a computer, or tap You (your profile picture, bottom right) in the app and tap View all next to Playlists. If you mean getting your video onto other people's playlists, only a playlist's owner, or people they invite to collaborate, can add videos to it.

Where are my saved playlists on YouTube?

Saved playlists sit in the same Playlists page as the ones you made. On a computer, open youtube.com/feed/playlists and click the Saved filter to show only playlists you saved from other channels, or Owned to show only your own.

Why doesn't my playlist show up when I click Save?

YouTube's Save to playlist menu loads at most 200 playlists. I confirmed the cap by reading YouTube's own API: playlist 201 is not further down, it is not in the list at all. The 200 it does show appear to be your most recently updated ones, so an old playlist can exist and still be missing from the menu. Playlists you saved from other people never appear there either, because you can't add videos to them.

Is there a YouTube playlist finder?

YouTube's own option is the Playlists page with its A-Z sort and Owned and Saved filters, plus Ctrl+F. I built a free Chrome extension, YouTube Playlist Search, whose Alt+P shortcut (Option+P on Mac) opens a search box over every playlist in your library and opens the one you pick. It works on desktop Chrome on youtube.com only, not on phones or YouTube Music.
