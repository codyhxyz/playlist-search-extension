How to search within a YouTube playlist

By Cody Hergenroeder, who builds YouTube Playlist Search and reads YouTube's playlist API for a living · Updated November 7, 2026

Quick answer

YouTube has no search box inside a playlist. On a computer, open the playlist, scroll to the very bottom so every video loads, then press Ctrl+F (⌘+F on a Mac) and type part of the video's title. If you don't know which playlist the video is in, open the video and click Save: the playlists that already have it are ticked.

Is there a way to search inside my YouTube playlists?

Not a built-in one, on youtube.com. A playlist page has a title, a Play all button, a sort menu on your own playlists, and the list of videos. No search field. The search bar at the top of the page searches all of YouTube, not the playlist you're looking at.

The one exception is YouTube Music. Since late 2025 it has been rolling out a Find in playlist option in a playlist's three-dot menu, which searches song titles, artists and albums. Reports say the rollout is gradual, so your account may not have it yet. It only helps with music: YouTube Music only shows the music videos from a playlist, so it can't search your "cooking" or "lectures" playlists.

Everywhere else, the browser's own find-in-page is the tool.

How to search a YouTube playlist with Ctrl+F Desktop

  1. Open the playlist on youtube.com in a desktop browser. If you're in the video player, click the playlist's title in the panel on the right to get to the full playlist page. Your own playlists are all at youtube.com/feed/playlists.
  2. Scroll to the bottom, and keep going until no more videos appear. YouTube loads a playlist about 100 videos at a time. Pressing End a few times is faster than the scroll wheel.
  3. Press Ctrl+F (⌘+F on a Mac) and type a distinctive word from the title, or the channel's name. Press Enter to jump from match to match.

That's it. For a playlist of a few hundred videos it takes under a minute.

Why doesn't Ctrl+F find a video I know is in the playlist?

Find-in-page searches the text on the page, nothing more. With a YouTube playlist that leaves real gaps:

Other ways to narrow a long playlist

Sort it first

On a playlist you own, the sort menu above the videos can order them by date added, by popularity, or by when each video was published. If you saved the video recently, sort by newest date added and it's near the top without any scrolling.

Search your watch history instead

If you watched the video, you don't need the playlist at all. youtube.com/feed/history has a Search watch history box on a computer, and it searches every video you've watched, playlist or not. This only works if watch history was turned on when you watched it.

Search YouTube, then check the Save menu

If you remember roughly what the video was, search all of YouTube for it, open it, and click Save. That tells you whether it's in your playlists, and which ones. Which brings up the second half of this problem.

How do I find a video I saved to a playlist, when I don't know which playlist?

Work backwards from the video. Open it, click Save (or the three-dot menu, then Save), and YouTube ticks every playlist in the menu that already contains it.

The catch with lots of playlists. YouTube's Save menu loads at most 200 of your playlists. I confirmed this by reading YouTube's own API on an account with over 250. If the video sits in playlist number 230, the menu shows every row unticked and that playlist isn't listed at all, so it looks like you never saved the video.

That gap is the part I fixed.

See every playlist a video is in, past 200

YouTube Playlist Search is a free Chrome extension that replaces the Save menu with a searchable one covering every playlist you own. It ticks each playlist that already has the video, including the ones past 200 that YouTube can't tell you about. Alt+P (Option+P on Mac) searches your playlists by name and opens the one you pick, so you can get to the right playlist and then Ctrl+F inside it.

To be clear about what it doesn't do: it doesn't search the video titles inside a playlist. Desktop Chrome on youtube.com only.

Can I search a YouTube playlist on my phone? Android iPhone

Not in the YouTube app. It has no search inside a playlist and no find-in-page. Two workarounds:

  • Use a mobile browser. Open the playlist on m.youtube.com in Chrome or Safari, scroll until every video has loaded, then use Find in page (Chrome: three-dot menu; Safari: the share button or the aA menu). It's fiddly with a long playlist, but it works.
  • Sort, then scroll. In the app, your own playlists have a sort option too. Newest date added first gets a recent save to the top.

For anything over a couple of hundred videos, a computer is worth walking to.

Questions people ask

Is there a way to search inside my YouTube playlists?

Not with a search box. A playlist page on youtube.com has no search of its own. On a computer, open the playlist, scroll until every video has loaded, then press Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on a Mac) and type part of the title. YouTube Music has been rolling out a Find in playlist option in a playlist's three-dot menu, but it only covers music.

How do I find a video I saved to a playlist?

If you know which playlist, open it, load every video by scrolling to the end, and press Ctrl+F. If you don't know which playlist, but watched the video, search your watch history at youtube.com/feed/history, open the video, and click Save: the playlists that already contain it are ticked. YouTube's Save menu only lists 200 playlists, so if you have more, a playlist past that point won't show up there.

Why doesn't Ctrl+F find a video that I know is in the playlist?

Because it hasn't loaded yet. YouTube loads a playlist about 100 videos at a time as you scroll, and find-in-page only sees what is on the page. Scroll to the very end first. Ctrl+F also only matches the title and channel name YouTube shows, not the description, and a video that was deleted or made private shows up under a placeholder title.

Can I search a YouTube playlist on my phone?

Not in the YouTube app, which has no search inside a playlist and no find-in-page. In a phone browser you can open the playlist on m.youtube.com and use the browser's Find in page option, after scrolling to load every video. A computer is much easier for a long playlist.

How do I find which playlist a video is in?

Open the video and click Save. YouTube ticks every playlist in the menu that already contains it. The menu only covers 200 of your playlists, so with more than that a playlist can be missing from the list. The free YouTube Playlist Search extension for Chrome shows the same ticks across every playlist you own, past 200.

Is there an extension that searches the videos inside a YouTube playlist?

Several extensions on the Chrome and Firefox stores claim to. I haven't tested them, so I can't vouch for any. My own extension, YouTube Playlist Search, does something adjacent: it searches your playlists by name and shows which of them already contain a video. It does not search the video titles inside a playlist.
